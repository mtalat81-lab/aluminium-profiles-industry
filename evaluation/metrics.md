**Document Version**: 1.0

## Overview

This document defines comprehensive performance metrics for evaluating and measuring skill effectiveness in the aluminum profiles industry.

---

## 1. Skill Performance KPIs

### Knowledge & Understanding

| Metric | Description | Target | Measurement Method |
|--------|-------------|--------|---------------------|
| **Accuracy Rate** | % of correct responses to knowledge assessments | ≥ 85% | Quizzes, tests, assessments |
| **Conceptual Understanding** | Depth of industry knowledge demonstrated | ≥ 80/100 | Expert evaluation, project review |
| **Best Practice Adherence** | Following industry standards and guidelines | ≥ 90% | Observation, documentation review |
| **Knowledge Retention** | Information retained after 30 days | ≥ 75% | Follow-up assessments |

### Practical Application

| Metric | Description | Target | Measurement Method |
|--------|-------------|--------|---------------------|
| **Skill Execution** | Ability to perform tasks independently | ≥ 85/100 | Hands-on evaluation, project completion |
| **Problem Solving** | Applying skills to real-world scenarios | ≥ 80/100 | Case study evaluation |
| **Efficiency** | Time to complete tasks vs. standard | ≥ 90% efficiency | Time tracking, benchmarking |
| **Quality Output** | Quality of delivered work | ≥ 90/100 | Quality review, peer assessment |

### Learning & Progression

| Metric | Description | Target | Measurement Method |
|--------|-------------|--------|---------------------|
| **Completion Rate** | % of users completing the skill | ≥ 80% | Enrollment tracking |
| **Time to Competency** | Hours/days to reach proficiency | < 20 hours (for intermediate) | Learning analytics |
| **Skill Progression** | Movement through skill levels | ≥ 70% advance to next level | Level assessments |
| **Engagement Score** | User engagement and participation | ≥ 80/100 | Platform analytics |

### User Satisfaction

| Metric | Description | Target | Measurement Method |
|--------|-------------|--------|---------------------|
| **User Satisfaction** | Overall satisfaction rating | ≥ 4.0/5.0 | Post-skill surveys |
| **Relevance Score** | Perceived industry relevance | ≥ 4.2/5.0 | User feedback |
| **Recommendation Likelihood** | Would recommend to others (NPS) | ≥ 50 | NPS survey |
| **Content Quality** | Perceived quality of materials | ≥ 4.0/5.0 | Content feedback surveys |

## 2. Supplier Benchmarking Metrics

### Production Excellence (Weight: 25%)

| Metric | Description | Measurement | Max Score |
|--------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| **Annual Capacity** | Tons per year production capacity | Current/planned capacity | 20 |
| **Production Lines** | Number of operational lines | Count of active extrusion lines | 15 |
| **Technology Level** | Manufacturing technology sophistication | Equipment assessment | 20 |
| **Energy Efficiency** | Energy consumption per ton produced | kWh/ton of output | 15 |
| **Uptime & Reliability** | Equipment operational reliability | % operational time | 20 |

**Category Score**: Average of weighted metrics (Target: ≥ 90/100)

### Quality Standards (Weight: 25%)

| Metric | Description | Measurement | Max Score |
|--------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| **Certifications** | Quality certifications held | ISO 9001, ISO 14001, etc. | 25 |
| **Quality Control** | Rigor of quality processes | QC protocol assessment | 20 |
| **Compliance Rate** | Regulatory/standard compliance | % compliance | 20 |

**Category Score**: Average of weighted metrics (Target: ≥ 90/100)

## 3. Variance Analysis Framework

### Variance Calculation

```
Variance % = ((Our Score - Industry Best) / Industry Best) × 100

Interpretation:
  0% = At par with industry leader
  -1% to -5% = Competitive, minor gap
  -6% to -15% = Needs improvement
  -16% to -30% = Significant gap
  < -30% = Major improvement needed
  > 0% = Exceeding industry best
```
